UC Santa Cruz, a place that boasts about its location in the woods and closeness to nature in an academic setting. This version Santa Cruz is home to many Pokémon, like the one you see on that bridge. The Pokémon you see here is called Goomy, the soft tissue Pokémon! It loves the rain and requires moisture to stay healthy. The school was built on Santa Cruz to study the surprising amount of biodiversity in the area, inspiring the Cruz-Dex add-on to the Pokédex app. Trainers are encouraged to learn how to catch Pokémon in the wilds of UCSC, and have developed their own Pokémon league in the process!
